2026-11-19 | Career preparation workflow
- Audience includes pre-final and final-year students, internship seekers,
  and people preparing for their next job.
- The candidate creates a profile, uploads a CV for extraction, and submits
  a target job description. The site scores CV fit, explains gaps, and
  provides job-specific CV improvements.
- The candidate takes a scored role-specific assessment, practices weak
  areas, and repeats the assessment until the readiness criteria are met.

- Open decisions include supported CV and job description formats, how
  candidates apply CV changes, assessment format, scoring rubrics, and
  the criteria for reporting readiness.
